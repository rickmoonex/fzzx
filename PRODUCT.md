# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Keyboard-first macOS users who compose small shell scripts for application launching, power controls, media controls, notes, and other quick actions. They need to choose one item without leaving their current workflow.

## Product Purpose

fzzx is a minimal stdin-driven fuzzy picker for macOS. It exists to provide the scriptability and immediacy of dmenu and Fuzzel while supporting native macOS text input and installed Nerd Fonts. Success means it appears quickly, stays out of the way, returns one exact result, and is easy to theme from scripts or one small config file.

## Brand Personality

Very simple, terse, and utilitarian. The interface should feel like dmenu: a temporary keyboard surface rather than a standalone application.

## Anti-references

Avoid rounded launcher cards, Raycast or Spotlight-style presentation, decorative effects, gradients, glass, animation, shadows, title bars, traffic lights, inset platform controls, and other native window chrome.

## Design Principles

- The tool disappears into the user's current task.
- Keyboard interaction is the complete workflow; pointer support is secondary.
- Script-facing behavior is stable, literal, and unsurprising.
- Density and clarity beat spaciousness and decoration.
- Native platform behavior is used only when it improves input, accessibility, or reliability.
- With no choices, the same surface acts as a minimal text prompt.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable high-contrast defaults, complete keyboard operation, native IME support, standard macOS editing shortcuts, and clear selected and matched states that do not depend on subtle color differences.
